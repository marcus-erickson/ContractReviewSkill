Contract Review Skill

A skill that reviews construction contracts. It runs each of the prompts in prompts.md against the contract and shows the full contract next to the results, with every point citing the section it comes from. Select a citation and the contract scrolls to that clause. When Claude publishes the page, an Ask Claude tab lets you ask your own questions about the contract (this static sample does not include it).
